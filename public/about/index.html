About me

I am a researcher and engineer by training. I am broadely interested in different kinds of problems and in the pursuit of which I end up learning from different traditional disciplines of knowledge.

I am at present associated with Information and Communication Technology for Development (ICTD) lab at IIT Delhi. Here I am working on building the Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) for ecological restoration and improving welfare of socially marginalised people. This project is led by Prof. Aaditeshwar Seth and is referred as CoRE Stack.

Previously I have worked on Neuroimaging Data Analysis, Wireless Communications Technologies, Foundation Models for Computer Networks, Power Systems, etc.

I am interested in mathematics, computing, engineering, and scientific research.

This website is a place where I write about things I am learning, problems I am working on, and ideas that I find interesting.

My interests span several areas, including mathematical analysis, computational methods, machine learning, neuroscience, power systems, and geospatial analysis.

What I am working on
