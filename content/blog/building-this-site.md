## A place to think

I don't want this website to be just a collection of links to projects and credentials.

I want it to be a place where I can write.

There are many things I encounter while studying mathematics, programming, engineering, and research that are worth understanding properly rather than simply solving once and forgetting.

Writing is one way of forcing myself to understand those things.

## A personal publication

The blog will therefore be the central part of this website.

Some articles will be technical. Some will be mathematical. Some may document projects or experiments.

Not everything needs to be a formal publication.

The goal is to build a record of things I have learned and worked on.

## Building with Hugo

I am using Hugo because the underlying system is relatively simple: Markdown files become HTML through templates.

That simplicity also makes the website itself an interesting project.
