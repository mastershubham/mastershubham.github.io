A Python-based hydrological analysis pipeline for processing large watershed datasets.

The pipeline uses DEM data together with GRASS GIS to perform sink filling, flow-direction and flow-accumulation analysis, stream extraction, watershed delineation, and stream-order computation.

The system is designed to work at substantially larger scales than a typical desktop GIS workflow.
